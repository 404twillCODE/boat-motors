Gas cans, gasoline & controls

Tank capacity: 9.9 HP: 2.5 gal · 20 HP: 4 gal · 25 HP: 5 gal · 50 HP: 8 gal · 100 HP: 12 gal.
Engine sounds: Each motor has its own engine loop. Pitch rises with throttle and speed. Adjust Minecraft’s Friendly Creatures sound slider to change engine volume.
Out of fuel? You can still paddle. A fully used gas can becomes an empty can; partly used cans keep their remaining gas. Recraft empty cans with coal, sugar and blaze powder.
Swap or remove: Right-click with a different motor to swap. Sneak-right-click with shears to remove. Fuel stays with the returned motor.
