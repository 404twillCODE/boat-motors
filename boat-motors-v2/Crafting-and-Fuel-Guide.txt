GAS CAN (makes 1 can containing 1 US gallon)
Combine one EMPTY GAS CAN, one COAL, one SUGAR and one BLAZE POWDER
anywhere in the crafting grid. Charcoal does not substitute for coal.
To make more gas, reuse the returned empty can in this same recipe.

GETTING INGREDIENTS
Iron/copper/gold: mine their ores and smelt the raw metals.
Redstone and diamonds: mine their ores with a suitable pickaxe.
Furnace: eight cobblestone around an empty center slot.
Amethyst shards: harvest fully grown amethyst clusters in a geode.
Netherite ingot: craft four netherite scraps with four gold ingots.
Coal: mine coal ore. Sugar: craft sugar cane into sugar.
Blaze powder: craft a blaze rod from a Nether fortress blaze into powder.

INSTALL, REFUEL AND DRIVE
1. Place a boat, chest boat, bamboo raft or chest raft in water.
2. Hold your motor and right-click the boat to attach it.
3. Hold a gas can and right-click the boat to add gas. Repeat to fill.
   New motors start empty. Unused gas remains in a partly used can;
   a completely used can becomes an empty gas can.
4. Board the boat. W accelerates, S brakes/reverses, A/D steer.
5. The display above the hotbar shows speed in MPH, HP, tank gallons
   and estimated miles per gallon. MPG changes with speed.
6. When gas runs out you can still paddle normally.

TANK SIZES
9.9 HP: 2.5 gallons    20 HP: 4 gallons    25 HP: 5 gallons
50 HP: 8 gallons      100 HP: 12 gallons

REMOVE OR CHANGE MOTORS
Sneak-right-click the boat with shears to remove its motor.
Right-click with a different motor to replace the current motor.
Remaining fuel stays with the returned motor.
